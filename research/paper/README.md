# Paper package

`outline.md` is the claim-disciplined outline and `manuscript.md` is the
evidence-complete manuscript prepared for human paper review. Numerical results
must be inserted only from `research/tables/` and figures from
`research/figures/`; both are generated from machine-readable evidence by
`research/analysis/generate_figures.py`.

The package includes the canonical five-model compatibility table and the
complete repeated M4 principal analysis. Optional M5 independent validation
was not executed, so its claims remain explicitly absent rather than blocking
the M1--M4 manuscript. `research/PUBLICATION_READINESS.md` is the review gate,
and `research/claim_boundaries.json` is the machine-readable claim policy.
